❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What can I use Split Bill Tools for?

Split Bill Tools provides a collection of tools for splitting bills, converting currencies, planning travel costs, managing group expenses, calculating business costs and organizing student and everyday living expenses.

What is the Currency Converter?

The Currency Converter lets you convert amounts between 100+ currencies. Select the currency you are converting from, choose the currency you want to convert to, enter an amount and calculate the conversion.

Exchange rates can change over time, so displayed conversions may differ from rates provided by banks, card providers or other financial institutions.

What can I do with Split Bill?

Split Bill includes tools for splitting bills, scanning receipts, copying and pasting receipt information, calculating percentage cuts and working with different currencies. You can review results and use the available save, share and delete options.

What is Advanced Bill Splitting?

Advanced Bill Splitting provides more flexible ways to divide a bill, including proportional and unequal splits, item-based splitting and calculations involving tax and tips.

What can I calculate with Travel Budget?

Travel Budget helps you plan and organize travel expenses. You can calculate trip costs, daily travel budgets, accommodation costs, food budgets, transportation costs and activities or entertainment expenses.

What is Group Expenses?

Group Expenses helps you manage shared costs for different situations, including roommate expenses, couples expenses, vacation expenses, road trips, events, weddings and group gifts. You can calculate how expenses should be divided between people or contributors.

What can I calculate with Business Expenses?

Business Expenses provides tools for common business-related costs, including corporate dinners, business travel, invoice splitting, freight costs, import costs and cost calculations based on weight or volume.

What is Student & Living Costs?

Student & Living Costs helps organize common housing and everyday living expenses. It includes tools for student housing, rent splitting, utility splitting, meal budgets and study abroad budgets.

Can I save my calculations?

Yes. Where saving is available, you can save your calculation and view it later in the saved history on the same device. You can also use the available share and delete options.

Where are my saved calculations stored?

Saved calculations are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our server. Your saved history is therefore available only on the device where it was saved.

Can I share my calculation?

Yes. Where the share option is available, you can share your calculation using the sharing features provided by your device or copy the result when device sharing is unavailable.

Can I edit receipt information after scanning?

Yes. After scanning or processing receipt information, review the detected items and prices carefully. Where editing controls are available, you can correct information, add missing items or remove incorrect items before using the final calculation.

Are currency exchange rates guaranteed?

No. Exchange rates are not guaranteed. Rates can change and may differ from those used by banks, payment providers, card issuers and other financial institutions.
