❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does the bill splitter work?

Enter the total bill and number of people. The calculator divides the bill equally and shows how much each person pays.

Can I scan a receipt?

Yes. You can take a photo or upload a receipt image and use the receipt tool to identify items and prices.

Can I copy and paste a receipt?

Yes. You can paste receipt text into the receipt tool and create an editable bill from the information.

Can I convert currencies?

Yes. The Currency Converter supports many currencies and allows you to search for the currency you need.

Can I share my calculation?

Yes. You can use the available sharing option on your device to share your calculation or receipt result.

Are currency exchange rates guaranteed?

No. Exchange rates can change and may differ from rates provided by banks, payment providers and other financial institutions.

How do I split a bill between different numbers of people?

Enter the total bill amount and the number of people sharing the bill. Split Bill Calculator divides the amount according to the number of people you enter and shows how much each person should pay.

How do I calculate a percentage of a bill?

Enter the bill amount and the percentage you want to calculate. For example, 30% of $120 is $36. The Percentage Cut tool calculates the percentage amount for you.

Can I add or remove receipt items?

Yes. After a receipt has been scanned, uploaded or processed from pasted text, you can review the itemized list. You can edit available items, add missing items or remove items that should not be included before checking the final receipt total.

What should I do if the receipt scanner misses an item?

Review the scanned receipt carefully. If an item or price is missing or incorrect, use the available editing controls to add or correct the information manually. Always check the edited receipt before relying on the calculated total.

How do I change the currency used for my bill?

Select your preferred currency from the currency selection option before calculating your bill. Choose the currency that matches the bill you are working with so the displayed amounts use the appropriate currency.

How do I use the currency converter?

Enter the amount you want to convert, select the From Currency and To Currency, and then calculate the conversion. You can also use the currency swap option to reverse the conversion direction.

Exchange rates can change over time, so the displayed conversion may differ from the rate used by your bank, card provider or other financial institution.

How can I check my calculation before paying?

Compare the calculator result with the original receipt before making a payment. Check the bill amount, selected currency, number of people, item prices and percentage calculations. If you scanned or uploaded a receipt, review the detected information and correct any mistakes before relying on the result.

Can I install Split Bill Calculator on my phone?

Yes. Split Bill Calculator can be installed as a Progressive Web App (PWA) on supported devices and browsers.

On supported Android devices, use the browser's available Install or Add to Home Screen option. On iPhone, use the browser's Share option and select Add to Home Screen when available.
