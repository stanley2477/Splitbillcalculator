About Us

Welcome to Split Bill Calculator, a free online utility website designed to make everyday calculations simple, convenient, and easy to understand.

Our goal is to provide practical tools that help users quickly calculate bills, work with percentages, process receipts, and convert currencies from one convenient place.

Our Tools

Split Bill Calculator provides several useful tools, including:

  • Bill Splitter: Divide a bill between multiple people and see how much each person should pay.
  • Percentage Calculator: Calculate a percentage of a bill and determine an individual share or percentage cut.
  • Receipt Scanner: Scan or upload a receipt image and convert detected items and prices into an editable bill.
  • Copy & Paste Receipt: Paste receipt text from another application and create an editable bill from the detected items and prices.
  • Currency Converter: Convert amounts between supported currencies using current exchange-rate data.

Our Purpose

We aim to make everyday financial calculations easier by providing simple, accessible, and responsive online tools that can be used on phones, tablets, and computers.

The tools are provided for general informational and calculation purposes. Users should verify important financial, payment, tax, or currency information with the appropriate source or qualified professional when necessary.
