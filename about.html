About Us

Welcome to Split Bill Calculator, a free online utility website designed to make everyday calculations, expense planning, and bill management simple and convenient.

Our goal is to provide practical online tools that help people calculate, split, organize, and understand different types of expenses from one convenient place.

Our Tools

Split Bill Calculator currently provides seven main tool sections covering everyday, travel, group, business, and student-related expenses.

💱 Currency Converter

Convert amounts between 100+ supported currencies. Select the currency you are converting from, choose the destination currency, and calculate the conversion using available exchange-rate data.

🧮 Main Calculator

The Main Calculator provides everyday bill calculation tools, including bill splitting, receipt scanning, receipt copy and paste, percentage calculations, currency selection, and available save, share, and delete options.

⚖️ Advanced Bill Splitting

Advanced Bill Splitting provides more flexible ways to divide expenses, including proportional and unequal splits, item-based splitting, tax, tips, and other customized bill calculations.

✈️ Travel Budget

Travel Budget helps users organize and plan trip-related expenses, including accommodation, food, transportation, daily travel costs, activities, and other travel spending.

👥 Group Expenses

Group Expenses helps users manage shared costs for roommates, couples, vacations, road trips, events, weddings, and group gifts. The tools help calculate how expenses can be divided between people or contributors.

💼 Business Expenses

Business Expenses provides tools for common business-related costs, including corporate dinners, business travel, invoice splitting, freight costs, import costs, and cost calculations based on weight or volume.

🎓 Student & Living Costs

Student & Living Costs helps organize housing and everyday living expenses, including student housing, rent splitting, utility splitting, meal budgets, and study-abroad budgets.

Our Purpose

We aim to make everyday expense calculations easier by providing simple, accessible, and responsive online tools that can be used on phones, tablets, and computers.

Whether you are splitting a restaurant bill, planning a trip, managing shared household expenses, calculating business costs, or organizing a student budget, our goal is to provide useful tools that are straightforward to understand and easy to use.

About the Website

Accuracy and Responsible Use

Our calculators are designed to provide helpful calculation and budgeting results, but users should review important results before using them for payments, financial decisions, business purposes, travel planning, or other important matters.

Currency exchange rates may change, and receipt scanning or text recognition may occasionally produce incorrect information. Always verify important information against the original source when necessary.
