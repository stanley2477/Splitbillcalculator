How to Use Split Bill Calculator

Split Bill Calculator provides simple online tools for splitting bills, calculating percentages, processing receipts, and converting currencies. This guide explains how to use the main features and check your results before making a payment.

How to Use Split Bill Calculator

1. Select Your Currency

Before calculating a bill, select the currency that matches the bill you are working with. Choosing the correct currency helps ensure that the amounts displayed by the calculator use the appropriate currency symbol.

2. How to Use the Bill Splitter

Enter the total amount of the bill and enter the number of people sharing the expense. The calculator divides the bill according to the number of people and shows the amount each person should pay.

3. How to Use Percentage Cut

Enter the bill amount and the percentage you want to calculate. The calculator determines the percentage amount and displays the result so you can understand how much the selected percentage represents.

4. How to Scan a Receipt

Use the receipt scanning feature to take a clear photo of a physical receipt. The receipt scanner uses optical character recognition (OCR) to identify text and possible item prices. After scanning, review the detected information carefully because unclear or damaged receipts may produce incorrect results.

5. How to Upload a Receipt

If you already have a picture of your receipt on your phone, use the receipt upload option to select the image from your device. After the receipt is processed, review the detected items and prices before using the calculated total.

6. How to Copy & Paste Receipt Text

If you have a digital receipt, copy the receipt text from your email, messaging application, or another source and paste it into the receipt text field. The tool can process the text and identify item names and prices where the receipt format is supported.

7. How to Edit, Add, or Remove Receipt Items

After a receipt has been scanned, uploaded, or pasted, review the itemized information. If an item or price is incorrect, use the available editing controls to correct it. You can also add missing items or remove items that should not be included before checking the final receipt total.

8. How to Use the Currency Converter

Enter the amount you want to convert. Select the currency you are converting from and the currency you want to convert to. The converter then displays the corresponding converted amount using the available exchange-rate data.

Exchange rates can change over time, so the displayed conversion should be treated as an estimate rather than a guarantee of the exact amount your bank or payment provider will charge.

9. How to Share Your Result

After completing your calculation, use the Share Result feature when available. Your device can then provide the available sharing options, allowing you to send the calculation result to other people.

10. How to Check Your Result Before Paying

Before making a payment, compare the calculator result with the original receipt. Check the bill amount, selected currency, number of people, item prices, and any percentage calculation. If you scanned or uploaded a receipt, make sure the detected information is correct.

11. How to Install Split Bill Calculator on Your Phone

Split Bill Calculator can be installed as a Progressive Web App (PWA) on supported devices and browsers.

On supported Android devices, use the browser's available Install or Add to Home Screen option. On iPhone, use the browser's Share option and select Add to Home Screen when available.

Scanned Receipt Example

  • Jollof Rice — $18.00
  • Chicken — $15.00
  • Drink — $7.00

Always review the scanned items and correct any mistakes before using the result.

Uploaded Receipt Example

Select a receipt image already saved on your phone. The receipt scanner processes the uploaded image and attempts to identify the items and prices.

Review the extracted information and make any necessary corrections before calculating or splitting the bill.

Copy & Paste Receipt Example

Copy receipt text and paste it into the receipt text tool.

  • Steak — $25
  • Chicken — $15
  • Drink — $10

The tool can process the receipt text and calculate the identified items.

Example total: $50

Currency Selection Example

Suppose you are calculating a restaurant bill in Canada. Select CAD — Canadian Dollar before entering the bill amount.

If the bill is $200 CAD, the calculator will display the amount using the selected Canadian Dollar currency.

Currency Conversion Example

Suppose you have an amount in USD and want to see its value in GBP. Enter the amount, select USD as the From Currency, select GBP as the To Currency, and calculate the conversion.

The displayed result is based on the available exchange-rate data at the time of conversion. Exchange rates may change.

Share Result Example

After completing a bill calculation, select Share Result. Your device's available sharing options can then be used to send the result to friends, family, or other people involved in the bill.

Important Reminder

Receipt scanning and text recognition can occasionally make mistakes, especially when a receipt is blurry, folded, faded, or poorly lit. Always review the information and compare the final result with the original receipt before paying.
