How to Use Split Bill Calculator

Split Bill Calculator provides a collection of online tools for splitting bills, converting currencies, planning travel costs, managing group expenses, calculating business costs, and organizing student and everyday living expenses. This guide explains the main features and how to use them.

Our Main Features

💱 1. Currency Converter

The Currency Converter allows you to convert amounts between 100+ supported currencies.

  1. Enter the amount you want to convert.
  2. Select the currency you are converting from.
  3. Select the currency you want to convert to.
  4. Calculate the conversion.

Exchange rates can change over time. The displayed conversion may differ from rates provided by banks, card providers, or other financial institutions.

🧮 2. Main Calculator

The Main Calculator provides everyday bill calculation tools, including bill splitting, receipt processing, percentage calculations, currency selection, and result management.

Bill Splitter

Enter the total bill and the number of people sharing the expense. The calculator divides the amount and shows how much each person pays.

Receipt Scanner

Take or upload a clear receipt image. The receipt scanner uses OCR to identify text and possible item prices. Always review the detected information before relying on the result.

Copy & Paste Receipt

Copy receipt information from a digital source and paste it into the receipt tool. The tool processes the text and identifies supported item names and prices.

Percentage Cut

Enter a bill amount and the percentage you want to calculate. The calculator displays the corresponding percentage amount.

⚖️ 3. Advanced Bill Splitting

Advanced Bill Splitting provides more flexible ways to divide a bill when an equal split is not suitable.

Depending on the available calculator, you can work with proportional or unequal splits, item-based expenses, taxes, tips, and other customized bill-sharing calculations.

Review the entered amounts, percentages, taxes, tips, and assigned items before using the final result.

✈️ 4. Travel Budget

Travel Budget helps you organize the expected costs of a trip and create a clearer travel spending plan.

You can work with travel costs such as accommodation, food, transportation, activities, daily spending, and other trip expenses.

Enter the relevant costs and review the calculated total or budget before using the result for your travel planning.

👥 5. Group Expenses

Group Expenses helps manage shared costs between friends, family, roommates, couples, travelers, coworkers, and other groups.

It includes tools for:

  • Roommate Expenses
  • Couples Expenses
  • Vacation Expenses
  • Road Trip Expenses
  • Event Expenses
  • Wedding Expenses
  • Group Gift Expenses

Enter the applicable expense amount and the people or contributors involved. The calculator then determines how the cost should be divided.

💼 6. Business Expenses

Business Expenses provides calculators for common business-related costs and shared expenses.

It includes:

  • Corporate Dinner
  • Business Travel
  • Business Invoice Splitter
  • Freight Cost
  • Import Cost
  • Cost-per-Weight/Volume

Enter the required business cost information and use the calculator to determine the relevant total, split, or cost calculation.

🎓 7. Student & Living Costs

Student & Living Costs helps students and individuals organize housing, rent, utilities, meals, and study-abroad expenses.

It includes:

  • Student Housing
  • Rent Split
  • Utilities Split
  • Meal Budget
  • Study Abroad Budget

Enter the applicable costs and use the calculator to organize your budget and understand your expected expenses.

Currency Selection

Many calculators allow you to select the currency used for your calculation. Search for the currency or country, then select the appropriate currency before calculating your expense.

Example: If you are calculating an expense in Canadian Dollars, select CAD — Canadian Dollar before entering or calculating the amount.

Saving, Sharing and Deleting Results

Save a Calculation

Where saving is available, select the Save option after calculating a result. Saved calculations can be viewed later in the saved history on the same device.

Share a Calculation

Where sharing is available, select the Share option. Your device can provide available sharing options or copy the result when device sharing is unavailable.

Delete a Result

Use the available Delete option to remove a displayed result or a saved calculation. Saved history also provides an option to clear all saved calculations.

Where Are Saved Calculations Stored?

Saved calculations are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our server. Your saved history is therefore available only on the device where it was saved.

Checking Your Results

Always review your calculation before making a payment or using the result for financial planning. Check the selected currency, amounts, number of people, percentages, item information, and any other values you entered.

Receipt scanning and text recognition may occasionally produce incorrect information, especially when a receipt is blurry, folded, faded, damaged, or poorly lit. Always compare detected receipt information with the original receipt.

Important Reminder

Split Bill Calculator provides calculation and budgeting tools for general informational purposes. Results should be reviewed carefully before being used for payments, business decisions, travel planning, student budgeting, or other important financial decisions.
