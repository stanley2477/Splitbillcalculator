1. Introduction

Split Bill Calculator ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our website and its calculation tools.

Our website provides tools for bill splitting, currency conversion, receipt processing, travel budgeting, group expenses, business expenses, and student and living costs.

The Main Calculator may provide bill splitting, percentage calculations, receipt scanning, receipt copy-and-paste processing, currency selection, and result management features.

Receipt scanning may process an image in the browser using optical character recognition (OCR) to identify text and possible item or price information.

Receipt information that you scan, upload, or paste is intended to be used to generate a calculation on the website. We do not intentionally store receipt images or receipt information on our own server.

You should review scanned or automatically processed information before relying on a calculation because OCR and automated processing may contain errors.
