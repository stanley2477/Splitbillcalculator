Split Bill Calculator ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our bill-splitting calculator, percentage calculator, currency converter, receipt scanner, receipt copy-and-paste feature, and result-sharing features.

2. Calculator Information

The amounts, percentages, number of people, and other values that you enter into our calculators are processed in your web browser to provide the requested calculations. We do not intentionally collect, store, or maintain a database of these calculator values.

3. Receipt Scanner

Our receipt scanner allows you to take or upload an image of a receipt. The image is processed in your browser using optical character recognition (OCR) to identify text and possible item prices.

Receipt images and information extracted from them are intended to be processed locally in your browser. Split Bill Calculator does not intentionally store receipt images, extracted receipt information, or receipt items on our servers.

You are responsible for the information you choose to scan or upload and should avoid processing information you do not want to use with the feature.

4. Copy and Paste Receipt Feature
